# [birds](https://barreto.jp/birds)

_birds_ is an app in my series **touching grass**, where, in an attempt to bring more awareness to the nature around me, I build the apps that will help me be more engaged.

Over time, I've become increasingly curious about the birds around me. Even though I would not consider myself anything remotely close to a bird-watcher, I do enjoy their songs and calls. With the help of [Merlin Bird ID](https://merlin.allaboutbirds.org/), I developed, with time, an often-flawed skill to identify the bird I'm listening to while I'm taking a walk or riding my bicycle.

I am lucky enough that the Netherlands has a decent variety of species around, with crows and magpies almost everywhere, parakeets in the trees, seagulls next to the canals, and smaller robins and great tits more hidden in parks. This app (a web app, but installable on your phone (PWA)) is basically a game that trains you to identify backyard birds with the goal of being better at identifying them in real life. With enough experience, not only will you be able to know which species it is thanks to its song, but most importantly, you will notice their songs that before would've gone unnoticed.

### Some details about the app

The app will remember which birds you struggle to find, and it will also remember which species you tend to confuse with another. The trick is when you are asked to identify a Great Tit, next to a Robin or Blue Tit. Certain birds resemble others, which can make this game challenging.
By remembering this, the app will generate harder tests eventually. If this proves to be too complicated, you can always reset your practice.

I did not add any daily streak; I trust you can manage your own habits. Notifications and gamification to get us artificially hooked work, so perhaps it is something I'll consider for the future.

### Sounds / Images

The sounds and images on the app were manually selected from [commons.wikimedia.org](https://commons.wikimedia.org/) or [xeno-canto.org](https://xeno-canto.org). Please check out xeno-canto! Great platform to share and find recordings of any kind of bird, properly categorised.

The copyright on each asset was respected, and all credits are listed not only after practice, but also in the [credits section](https://barreto.jp/birds/credits).
